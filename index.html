    ⭐ExtPrint3r

    • takes like 2-3 minutes to do
    • i’m still trying to figure out how to make this consistent

    1. click here

    2. click “Click here to load it anyway”

    3. click “Disable” under blocksi

    4. highlight the extension url, copy it, open it in a new tab

    5. on the extension tab, click the toggle switch next to 'Allow access to file URLs'

    6. go back to the ExtPrint3r tab

    - remember all of step 7, your chromebook will be laggy while doing this

    7. click 'The Print3r', press ctrl+tab, and then spam the spacebar for 5 seconds.

    next, press ctrl+w and wait until the print dialog shows up and press cancel

    8. profit

    (your chromebook will be laggy for a bit at first, you may need to do it twice)
